About Dondero Construction and Renovation

A general contractor built around professionalism and full start-to-finish capability, serving Calgary, Alberta and the surrounding area.

Our Story

Construction done the right way, start to finish

Dondero Construction and Renovation was founded on a simple standard: do the job right, communicate honestly, and stand behind the work. We built the business to offer homeowners and rental property owners something that's harder to find than it should be — a contractor capable of handling a project completely, from the first framing stud to the final coat of paint.

That full start-to-finish capability — spanning construction, renovation, and rough & finish carpentry — means fewer handoffs, fewer surprises, and one team accountable for the outcome.
