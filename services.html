Our Services

Full-scope construction and renovation for homeowners and rental property owners — from framing to final finish, we handle it all under one roof.

01 — Full Project Scope

Start-to-Finish Construction

When you work with Dondero, you get one contractor accountable for the entire build — not a rotating cast of subcontractors and disconnected schedules. We manage your project from the ground up: site prep and framing through mechanical rough-ins, insulation, drywall, and final finishing.

  • Single point of contact from planning through completion
  • Framing, structural work, and full builds
  • Coordinated scheduling to keep your project moving
  • Clear, honest updates at every phase

02 — Renovate & Restore

Renovations

Whether you're updating a kitchen, finishing a basement, adding on to your home, or preparing a rental unit for new tenants, we bring the same attention to detail to every renovation — respecting your home, your timeline, and your budget.

  • Kitchen, bathroom, and basement renovations
  • Additions and structural updates
  • Rental property turnovers and upgrades
  • Interior and exterior renovation work

03 — Precision Craftsmanship

Rough & Finish Carpentry

Carpentry is where a project earns its reputation. From structural framing to the final piece of trim, our rough and finish carpentry work is built on precision — square corners, tight seams, and details that hold up over time.

  • Structural and rough framing
  • Trim, casing, and baseboard installation
  • Custom cabinetry and built-ins
  • Doors, windows, and finish carpentry details
